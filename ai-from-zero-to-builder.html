7-Day Immersive Curriculum

Step-by-Step From Novice to Builder

Every single day includes hands-on building, mentor demonstration, and an immediate deliverable for your portfolio.

Day 1: AI Basics & Productivity

5 OCT (MON)
  • Model Architecture From Zero: Demystify tokens, context windows, and how LLMs generate responses without technical jargon.
  • Prompt Engineering Frameworks: Master role assignment, few-shot examples, chain-of-thought reasoning, and system instructions.
  • Deliverable: Build your customized Personal AI Productivity Kit.

Day 2: Research & Documents

6 OCT (TUE)
  • Large PDF & Report Analysis: Extract structured data, citations, and critical insights from 100+ page documents in seconds.
  • 10x Academic Research: Compare sources, uncover logical contradictions, and synthesize literature effortlessly.
  • Deliverable: Build your personalized AI Research Assistant.

Day 3: Images & Visual Content

7 OCT (WED)
  • Photorealistic Generation: Control lighting, aspect ratios, character consistency, and composition.
  • Visual Brand Design: Produce branded social post graphics, presentation slide visuals, and hero artwork.
  • Deliverable: Assemble your custom AI Visual Content Pack.

Day 4: Video, Voice & Social

8 OCT (THU)
  • Short-Form Video Production: Generate video clips, kinetic motion, and storyboard sequences.
  • Natural AI Voice Narration: Clone or generate studio-quality human voiceovers with nuanced inflections.
  • Deliverable: Complete and publish your First Full AI Video Reel.

Day 5: Web & App Building

9 OCT (FRI)
  • Idea to Interactive Software: Conceptualize, wireframe, and assemble functional web tools without writing syntax.
  • No-Code AI Integration: Connect dynamic AI prompts to responsive user input forms and live interfaces.
  • Deliverable: Deploy your Live Web Application on the public web.

Day 6: AI Automation

10 OCT (SAT)
  • Eliminating Busywork: Connect webhooks, Google Sheets, Gmail, and messaging apps into automated triggers.
  • Multi-Step Workflow Routing: Automatically process incoming data, analyze with AI, and route notifications.
  • Deliverable: Deploy your First Live AI Automation Pipeline.

Day 7: Capstone AI Project & Graduation

11 OCT (SUN)
  • Capstone Project Studio: Build and polish a full original AI project combining your favorite tools with live 1-on-1 mentor guidance.
  • Graduation Showcase: Present your project, receive peer feedback, and celebrate your graduation with your official signed certificate.
  • Deliverable: Official Core Lab Certificate of Completion + GitHub / LinkedIn Portfolio Showpiece.
