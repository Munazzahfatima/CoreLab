# 🔬 Core Lab — AI Education & Bootcamps

**Empowering the next generation of builders, creators, and students with practical, real-world AI skills.**

Founded by FAST University graduates, **Core Lab** is an educational platform dedicated to making Artificial Intelligence accessible, hands-on, and practical for everyone.

---

## 💡 Our Story
What began as interactive AI workshops in university classrooms has evolved into a structured learning platform. **Core Lab** bridges the gap between academic theory and practical application—teaching students and creators how to use AI to learn faster, automate routine work, create high-impact visual media, and build real digital projects.
