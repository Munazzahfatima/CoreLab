# 🔬 Core Lab — AI Education & Bootcamps

**Practical, hands-on AI education founded by FAST University graduates.**

Core Lab is an educational platform dedicated to making Artificial Intelligence accessible, hands-on, and practical for everyone. We cut the fluff, eliminate gatekeeping, and teach hands-on AI engineering through live, guided build sprints.

## 🏛️ Website Architecture (Multi-Page Platform)

The platform is structured into dedicated, modular pages:

1. [`index.html`](file:///index.html) — Modern landing page featuring interactive particle canvas, trust metrics, chronological course showcase ("NEXT UP" indicator), learning pathways, featured lab projects, mentor profiles, and floating WhatsApp support.
2. [`programs.html`](file:///programs.html) — Comprehensive programs hub with category filtering (All, Beginner, Advanced Engineering, Weekend) and an in-depth course comparison matrix.
3. [`ai-content-creator.html`](file:///ai-content-creator.html) — Dedicated 2-day weekend intensive syllabus, deliverables, and official registration form.
4. [`ai-from-zero-to-builder.html`](file:///ai-from-zero-to-builder.html) — Dedicated 7-day flagship bootcamp syllabus, 7 hands-on projects, capstone deliverables, and registration form.
5. [`data-science-machine-learning.html`](file:///data-science-machine-learning.html) — Dedicated 10-day technical bootcamp syllabus, Streamlit/GitHub outcomes, tiered pricing, and enrollment.
6. [`generative-ai-agents.html`](file:///generative-ai-agents.html) — Dedicated 10-day advanced AI engineering bootcamp syllabus covering RAG pipelines, LangChain, autonomous multi-agent systems, and production deployment.
7. [`projects.html`](file:///projects.html) — Course Projects & Student Capstones: *"Real AI Projects You Will Build, Deploy & Own."* Featuring portfolio capstones across Creator, Builder, Data Science & Machine Learning, and Autonomous AI Agent tracks.
8. [`about.html`](file:///about.html) — Origin story from FAST-NUCES university labs, builder-first ethos, instructor profiles, and certificate standard.
9. [`faq.html`](file:///faq.html) — Interactive categorized FAQ accordion covering General, Programs, Technical Tools, Registration/Payment, and Certificates.
