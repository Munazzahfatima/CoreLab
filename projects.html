Ongoing Computer Vision & IoT

FabriSense — Embedded AI Sensing

A high-accuracy automated quality inspection system engineered as a FAST-NUCES final year research initiative. Combines custom computer vision classification models with sensory edge computing hardware to detect microscopic textile defects, weave inconsistencies, and surface flaws in real time on industrial manufacturing lines.

Ongoing Multimodal AI & NLP

BLIP Multimodal Vision-Language App

Full-stack multimodal vision-language system leveraging Salesforce BLIP (Vision Transformer ViT-B/16 image encoder paired with a BERT language decoder). Generates rich natural language descriptions for arbitrary imagery, supporting both unconditional caption generation and prompt-guided conditional text generation with an asynchronous FastAPI backend and React frontend.

Ongoing TinyML & Wearable AI

SmartBand-AI — On-Device TinyML

Low-power embedded AI classifying 7 distinct daily human physical activities directly from multi-axis inertial measurement unit (IMU) sensor streams. Deployed completely on-device to an Arduino Nano 33 BLE microcontroller via Edge Impulse, ensuring zero cloud dependency, absolute data privacy, sub-millisecond response latency, and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) telemetry.

Arduino Nano 33 BLE TinyML Edge Impulse IMU Sensors BLE Embedded C++
