Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Core Lab programs, eligibility, and registration.

What is Core Lab and who is it for? +
Core Lab is a practical AI education initiative founded by FAST University graduates. It is specifically designed for beginners, school & university students, creators, professionals, and curious parents who want real, usable AI superpowers without confusing academic jargon.
What are the exact dates for each program? +
We have two upcoming programs in Autumn 2026:
  • AI Content Creator Weekend: Saturday & Sunday, 19 & 20 September 2026 (Intensive 2-day workshop).
  • AI From Zero to AI Builder (Cohort 5): Monday to Sunday, 5–11 October 2026 (Complete 7-day flagship cohort).
Do I need prior coding or technical knowledge to join? +
No prior programming or computer science experience is needed. Both programs start from scratch. We teach using intuitive visual tools, plain-English prompt engineering, and visual web builders that anyone with basic computer literacy can easily master.
Will I receive a verified certificate after completing the program? +
Yes! All students who complete the hands-on project milestones receive an official Core Lab Certificate of Completion signed by FAST University graduates, which you can showcase on your CV, LinkedIn, or student portfolio.

Click any "Register" or "View Details & Register" button on this page. It opens the official Google Form where you can submit your details and view simple payment instructions (via Easypaisa, JazzCash, or online bank transfer). Your seat will be confirmed via WhatsApp/Email.
