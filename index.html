Frequently Asked Questions

Core Lab is a practical AI education initiative founded by FAST University graduates to teach hands-on AI tools, visual creation, productivity, and web building to Pakistani students and creators.

The AI Content Creator Weekend takes place on 12 & 13 September 2026 (Saturday & Sunday). The 7-Day Cohort 5 runs from 5–11 October 2026 (Monday to Sunday).

Do students or parents need technical experience to join?

No prior coding or technical experience is required! Both programs are designed step-by-step for beginners, students, creators, and parents.
