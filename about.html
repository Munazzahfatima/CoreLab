The Genesis

From University Labs to Real-World Impact

During our engineering studies at FAST-NUCES, one truth became glaringly clear: university curricula excel at teaching mathematical foundations and algorithmic proofs, but often leave students unprepared to build, ship, and deploy modern AI systems.

Meanwhile, self-learning was plagued by superficial 10-minute tutorials showing pre-baked notebooks without explaining how APIs actually authenticate, how autonomous agents maintain persistent state, or how machine learning models are deployed into production web apps.

What started as interactive university workshops rapidly grew. We founded Core Lab to eliminate gatekeeping and teach hands-on AI engineering through live, guided build sprints. Whether you are creating automated content workflows or engineering autonomous agents, you leave with real software that works.
